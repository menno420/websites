Mockup The Projects overview, redrawn so each Project shows its state

A proposal, not a screenshot. On 2026-07-11 the owner's claude.ai Projects overview showed eight cards — a name, an age ("yesterday", "2 days ago") and "Only visible to you" — and a sidebar with two Routines and the same eight Projects. Nothing on it said whether a Project was working, stalled, or waiting on him. His words: "This screen is where I'd like the projects to be showing whether or not they are active." Below is that screen redrawn with the state he asked for. The names, ages and Routine names are what his screen showed; every state, time and count is an illustrative value — an example of what the screen would carry, not a measurement of anything.
